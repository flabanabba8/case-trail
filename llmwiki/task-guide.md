# Case Trail: sources, setup and worked research flow

Source-reviewed2026-11-10. See [exact schemas](contracts.json). This is a research
aid, not authenticated legal status or independent review.

## Choose from real resources

For offline/no-account work, selected UTF-8 opinion files and the supplied-source
tools need no model/service. Use an available native document extractor for PDFs;
retain page/source provenance and check extraction/OCR. Images are inspected in
the main session through an appropriate native interface. Do not infer text from
unseen scans. For online research, choose actual source access and the active
host's supported search/browser tools. Justia search and OpenJurist public fetching
are explicit existing adapters. Their source/URL restrictions do not apply to the
new supplied-source analysis/review tools. No private credentials are needed for
offline text analysis, and no repository URL should be fabricated.

## Worked source-based task

A user has an original opinion and a later one relevant to a specific notice
proposition. Preserve the two selected files. Give caselaw_examine_source_file the
later file, a descriptive source_label and the relevant case/citation identifiers.
Inspect its returned passages and surrounding source. A treatment word is a lead;
read the full relevant decisions, roles and contrary passages before interpreting
what changed.

Supply caselaw_review_sources with target/later objects containing label plus
exactly one of text or source_path. Optional URLs describe actual sources only.
Provide exact quote offsets from both opinions, the proposition, relationship,
treatment, extent, opinion role, courts, authority analysis, chronology when known,
identity rationale, outcome, supporting/contrary analysis and concrete limitations.
The existing rules still reject, for example, a judgment reversal presented as
unrelated later treatment or an overruling asserted from a dissent. These are
mechanical consistency checks, not proof that the proposed analysis is sound.

With save:true, keep the private receipt. caselaw_review_check_sources compares
selected current files against its fingerprints; changed/unavailable files need
another review. Saved labels and text are private task data, not a shipped wiki
corpus. Keep assistant/human and full-opinion/passages-only reading claims honest.

## Diagnose and accept

Missing paid/repository access: use sources actually available; if none suffice,
record that exact gap rather than claim complete research. A local document has
no URL: provide its label and file, not an invented Justia link. Missing/altered
quotes: recover complete source context and re-evaluate the claim. An unchanged
hash establishes text identity only, not provenance, current law or correctness.
A search/extraction failure remains a real failure; never change it into a clean
case-status result.

Accept a traceable proposed research trail with explicit scope, actual source
passages, unresolved authority/identity questions and retained original evidence.
Do not call a small corpus or a self-attested review comprehensive legal clearance.
